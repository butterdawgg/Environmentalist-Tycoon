# Environmentalist Tycoon
**Environmentalist Tycoon** is a 3D top-down tycoon game where you build your own sustainable energy network. Start with wind power and research labs, earn money by selling energy, and invest in research to unlock new technologies.

Wind → Solar → Biofuel → Nuclear

Manage your resources, optimize your territory, and build the most efficient and profitable energy network you can.

- Procedurally generated 32×32 maps
- Resource and research management
- Multiple energy technologies
- Stylized low-poly 3D graphics
- Build, demolish, and redesign your network freely
